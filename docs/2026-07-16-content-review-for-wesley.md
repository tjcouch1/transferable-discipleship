# TD App Content Changes — Review for Wesley

Hi Wesley! We're bringing all the changes from your working copy into the
latest version of the app. Before we finish, please look this over — it lists
everything that was **removed**, **added**, and **changed** in the app's
content, so you can confirm it all matches what you intended. Your working
copy was built up over several AI sessions, and AI tools sometimes do things
beyond what was asked, so this is a safety check.

The questions we most need answers to are at the top. Full details follow.

---

## ❓ Please confirm these (most important)

1. **The "How to use this app" page was deleted and nothing replaced it.**
   It was the page explaining the format: *"1. Read the question. 2. Read what
   the Bible says to answer the question. 3. Discuss each question based on the
   Scripture given,"* plus the note about the hints under each passage.
   The new "Why use TD" section talks about the purpose of TD but doesn't
   explain *how to use* the material. Was deleting this intentional? Should
   those instructions come back somewhere (e.g., inside "Why use TD")?

2. **The entire "Essentials" area is gone.** That includes:
   - *The Great Commission* and its 9 sub-pages (What is discipleship?, Go
     Make, Disciples, All Nations, Baptizing, Teaching, Empowered, God causes
     growth, Cycles of discipleship)
   - *Discipleship (Who, Why, & How)* and its 4 sub-pages (The motivation,
     Who?, Why?, How?)

   Your chat session shows you asked for this removal, so we believe it's
   intentional — just confirm, since this content will no longer exist
   anywhere in the app. (Note: "What is discipleship?" survives in a new form
   under The Challenge.)

3. **All five Basics lessons were completely rewritten.** The old versions of
   Gospel Review, Scripture 1.0, Assurance of Salvation, Holy Spirit, and
   Prayer are fully replaced (see the before/after page lists below). Confirm
   the new versions are the ones you want — the old wording won't be in the
   app anymore.

4. **Reflection questions will become styled text instead of buttons.**
   Right now, questions like *"Which of these benefits feels most needed in
   your life right now?"* look like tappable blue buttons, but tapping them
   does nothing except add "(tap to go back)" to the text. We're going to
   display them as the same highlighted box, just not tappable. Tap-to-reveal
   questions with real answers behind them stay tappable, exactly as you set
   them up.

5. **The old About page text moved.** The "Vision / Simple / Accessible /
   Transferable" write-up that used to live on the About page was replaced by
   the new "Why use TD" section (with new wording). The About page now has the
   app credits, the Dark Mode switch, and the Reset Progress button.
